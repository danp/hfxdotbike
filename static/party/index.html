Let's celebrate our community at the

Neighbourhood Block Party!

Vincent Street between Devonshire Ave and Young St

Sunday, September 20 12:00 PM to 3:00 PM Rain date: September 27

BBQ, music, games and good times!

What to expect

  • The street will be closed to through traffic 11am-4pm. Emergency access will be maintained. If you have a vehicle parked on the street, we kindly ask that you move it to a driveway or alternative street.
  • The party will run 12-3pm.
  • Free food, live music, activities for all ages. Bring friends and family!
  • If possible, bring your own plate to reduce waste.
  • Kids will be selling artwork, lemonade etc. for good causes. Bring a bit of cash!
  • Bring your own seating to use in the street or the pocket park.
  • No washrooms on site. The nearest public washroom is at Fort Needham park near the Needham St entrance.
  • If the party needs to be rescheduled due to weather, we will decide by 8am on Sept 20 and post on the website.

Questions? Email party@hfx.bike.
