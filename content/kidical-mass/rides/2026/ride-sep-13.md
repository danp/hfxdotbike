## Ride Specific Info

* Our meeting point is Chebucto Rd Park, located behind the Maritime Conservatory & Maritime Muslim Academy
* You can access the playground from Chebucto Rd, or from St. Matthias St. (near the Ummah Masjid and Community Centre)
* There are bus stops nearby on North St and Windsor St
* There is **no parking available in the Maritime Conservatory lot.** There is plenty of on-street parking on nearby streets.
* Chebucto Rd. Park has a small playground, benches, and a field, however there are no public washrooms available.
* **Please be cautious when riding through the parking lot to get to the playground.** The lots are quite busy due to a church service and dance classes at the Conservatory on Sunday mornings.
* Our destination is the playground at Le Marchant St Thomas School, and we’ll ride there using city streets.
* See the map below for more details of the route
* The school grounds at Le Marchant St Thomas have: benches & picnic tables for seating, multiple playground sites, and a large sports field. However, there are no public washrooms available at the school.
* We’ll provide snacks and prizes at the destination
* [Halifax Cycles](https://halifaxcycles.com/) will be offering family e-bike test rides at the end of the ride.
