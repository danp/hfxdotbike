faq:
  - question: How do the counters work?
    answer: Most bike counters use embedded sensors to detect and count bikes passing a fixed point. [Eco-Counter has a good overview](https://www.eco-counter.com/blog/bike-counters-how-do-they-work/). All current Halifax counters shown here are ZELT counters.
  - question: Who maintains the counters?
    answer: The current public counters shown here are maintained by Halifax Regional Municipality.
  - question: Who does BikeHfxStats?
    answer: BikeHfxStats is put together by [Dan](https://danp.net/), who collects the data into this site and the regular stats posts.
  - question: Do the counters count people or bikes?
    answer: Just bikes. For observations that include people and other details about how people travel, see [Pedal Poll](https://velocanadabikes.org/pedalpoll/).
